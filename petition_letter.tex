\section*{IV. PRESERVATION OF LEGAL ARGUMENT}

I respectfully preserve the following argument for the record:

The statutory text at 8 U.S.C. § 1153(b)(1)(A) and implementing regulation at 8 C.F.R. § 204.5(h)(3) establish that satisfaction of three criteria constitutes sufficient evidence of extraordinary ability. Having met six criteria, I satisfy the regulatory standard.

In \textit{Mukherji v. Miller}, Case No. 4:24-cv-03170 (D. Neb. Jan. 28, 2026), the U.S. District Court for the District of Nebraska ruled that USCIS's ``final merits determination'' framework—which permits denial even after criteria are met—was \textbf{unlawfully adopted} without Administrative Procedure Act (APA) rulemaking. The court found this practice arbitrary, capricious, and contrary to law, vacating the denial and ordering approval.

While \textit{Mukherji} is not binding in the Third Circuit, it constitutes highly persuasive authority on the APA violation inherent in applying extra-regulatory standards through the final merits determination. The court's analysis applies to all circuits as a matter of federal administrative law.

I respectfully request that USCIS approve this petition based on satisfaction of the regulatory criteria, consistent with the statute, regulations, and recent federal court precedent. A comprehensive legal analysis is included in the accompanying petition.
