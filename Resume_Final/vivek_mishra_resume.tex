\sect{Professional Profile}

{\small\color{bodytext}
Vivek Kumar Mishra is a Senior Software Engineer at \textbf{The Vanguard Group} (Aug 2026--present),
one of the world's largest investment management firms with \$9+ trillion AUM. A distinguished
scholar-practitioner with \textbf{12+ years} of contributions spanning enterprise technology, U.S.
federally funded research, and international academic scholarship, he holds the title of
\textbf{Fellow of the British Computer Society (FBCS)}---the most prestigious grade conferred
by the UK's Chartered Institute for IT---and is an \textbf{IEEE Senior Member}, held by fewer
than 8\% of IEEE's 460,000+ global members. Prior to Vanguard, he served as Founding Engineer
at \textbf{JPMorgan Chase \& Co.} (Oct 2024--May 2026) on systems serving 80+ million Americans,
delivering \$100K+ in savings and a 43\% performance improvement. He is also the recipient of the
\textbf{2024 Global Recognition Award} from an independent awarding body. His federally funded research at \textbf{California State
University, Long Beach} has been permanently archived by the U.S. Department of Transportation
and adopted by the Florida DOT. He is the author of 9 peer-reviewed publications (48+ citations,
h-index 4) and the textbook \textit{Design Patterns for AI Agents} (ISBN: 979-8268912371),
adopted into AI curricula at three independent AICTE-regulated engineering institutions.
He serves as peer reviewer for \textbf{NeurIPS}, \textbf{ICLR}, and \textbf{ICML} (all CORE
A*) and has been featured in publications reaching tens of millions of readers globally.
}
