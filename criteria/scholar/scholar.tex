\subsubsection{Solving Critical Infrastructure Financing: California High-Speed Rail}
\label{scholar:hsr}

\textbf{2021-2022: Evaluating Innovative Financing Mechanisms for the California High-Speed Rail Project}

\dr addressed one of the most significant infrastructure financing challenges in American history: the California High-Speed Rail (HSR) project, a \$105 billion investment to connect Los Angeles and San Francisco. The project faces a multi-billion dollar funding gap that threatens its completion.

His research, funded by Senate Bill 1 and the U.S. DOT through the California State University Transportation Consortium, employed advanced optimization algorithms (specifically TOPSIS) to evaluate and prioritize HSR stations for transit-oriented development. By analyzing large datasets of property values, accessibility metrics, and workforce demographics, \dr provided data-driven recommendations for sustainable funding strategies.

\textbf{Key Findings with Direct Policy Applicability:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Stations such as San Francisco Transbay Terminal, University City, and Downtown Merced should be prioritized for development
    \item Transit-oriented development can generate property tax revenues to partially offset construction costs
    \item AI-driven optimization can identify investment priorities that maximize economic return
\end{itemize}

Dr. Shailesh Chandra, Associate Professor at California State University, Long Beach, who supervised this research, explains the policy significance:

\begin{quote}
``\textit{These contributions advanced how transportation agencies like Caltrans assess and simulate investment impacts and mobility strategies. His innovations helped bridge traditional transportation models with modern AI tools, improving real-time decision-making and modeling for system resilience, equity, and efficiency.}'' (Dr. Shailesh Chandra, Associate Professor, California State University, Long Beach) [Exhibit LOR-1]
\end{quote}

This research has been cited in international studies examining high-speed rail financing, including research from Italy and Iran, demonstrating that \drs AI-driven financing methodologies have global applicability.
