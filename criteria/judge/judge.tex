\textbf{ICLR 2025 FM-Wild Workshop (International Conference on Learning Representations)}

The International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR) is universally recognized as one of the top-tier venues for machine learning research, alongside NeurIPS and ICML. Founded in 2013 by Yoshua Bengio and Yann LeCun, two pioneers of deep learning (LeCun received the 2018 Turing Award), ICLR has rapidly become the premier venue for research on neural networks, representation learning, and artificial intelligence. The conference uses an open peer review process through OpenReview, where reviews and discussions are publicly visible, ensuring transparency and high standards in the evaluation process.

The FM-Wild (Foundation Models in the Wild) Workshop at ICLR 2025 focuses on the deployment and real-world applications of large-scale foundation models including Large Language Models (LLMs) and multimodal AI systems. This workshop specifically addresses the challenges of deploying AI systems in production environments---a topic directly aligned with \drs expertise from his work at JPMorgan Chase.

\textbf{NeurIPS 2025 Position Paper Track (Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems)}

Established in 1987, NeurIPS has grown from a specialized academic gathering to the world's largest and most influential conference on artificial intelligence, attracting researchers from leading universities (MIT, Stanford, Berkeley, CMU, Oxford, Cambridge) and major technology companies (Google, Meta, Microsoft, OpenAI, DeepMind).

The Position Paper Track at NeurIPS 2025 is a specialized track that solicits papers taking a stance on important open questions in machine learning, including policy considerations, philosophical perspectives on AI, and forward-looking visions for the field. This track requires reviewers with broad knowledge of the AI landscape and the ability to evaluate not only technical merit but also the significance and potential impact of proposed positions.

The Program Chairs and Area Chairs curate the reviewer pool to ensure that only researchers with significant expertise are entrusted with evaluating submissions that could shape the future direction of AI research.
