\textbf{NeurIPS 2025 Position Paper Track (Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems)}

NeurIPS (originally NIPS, renamed in 2018) is a premier international conference on machine learning and computational neuroscience, consistently ranked among the top venues in AI/ML by virtually all academic ranking systems. Established in 1987, NeurIPS has grown from a specialized academic gathering to one of the world's largest and most influential conferences on artificial intelligence, attracting researchers from leading universities (MIT, Stanford, Berkeley, CMU, Oxford, Cambridge) and major technology companies (Google, Meta, Microsoft, OpenAI, DeepMind).

The Position Paper Track at NeurIPS 2025 is an official track introduced to solicit papers taking a stance on important open questions in machine learning, including policy considerations, philosophical perspectives on AI, and forward-looking visions for the field. Unlike standard research papers that report accomplished technical advances, position papers argue for viewpoints on what \textit{should} be done, addressing the ethical, societal, and strategic implications of AI development. NeurIPS describes this track as a ``springboard for public discussion'' on timely topics that require input from the machine learning community. Position papers accepted to this track are presented in oral and poster sessions at the main NeurIPS conference, integrating these critical discussions into the broader conference program. This track requires reviewers with broad knowledge of the AI landscape and the ability to evaluate not only technical merit but also the significance and potential impact of proposed positions on the future direction of the field.
