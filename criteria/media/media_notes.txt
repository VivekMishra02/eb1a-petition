## EVIDENCE FOR 8 CFR § 204.5(h)(3)(iii): PUBLISHED MATERIAL ABOUT THE BENEFICIARY IN PROFESSIONAL OR MAJOR TRADE PUBLICATIONS OR OTHER MAJOR MEDIA

---

### I. REGULATORY FRAMEWORK

Under 8 CFR § 204.5(h)(3)(iii), an alien may establish extraordinary ability through evidence of:

> "Published material about the alien in professional or major trade publications or other major media, relating to the alien's work in the field for which classification is sought. Such evidence shall include the title, date, and author of the material, and any necessary translation."

The Administrative Appeals Office (AAO) has clarified that this criterion requires:

1. **Published material** in professional, major trade publications, or major media;
2. The material must be **about the beneficiary** (not authored by the beneficiary);
3. The material must **relate to the beneficiary's work** in the field; and
4. Evidence of the **circulation and prominence** of the publishing outlet.

*See Matter of Chawathe*, 25 I&N Dec. 369 (AAO 2010); *Kazarian v. USCIS*, 596 F.3d 1115 (9th Cir. 2010).

The AAO has recognized that in scientific and technical fields, coverage in major professional publications (such as ACM, IEEE) and leading trade publications carries substantial weight, particularly when the beneficiary is quoted as an expert providing commentary on emerging technologies and policy matters. *See* AAO decisions in cases involving artificial intelligence researchers and computer science professionals.
