{\Large \textbf{NEWS TITLE: GUIDANCE PROVIDED ON CAREER OPPORTUNITIES IN COMPUTER SCIENCE}}
\end{center}

\vspace{0.3cm}

\textbf{Jaipur @ Patrika Plus.} Anand International College of Engineering organized a special lecture for students in the Computer Science field regarding career opportunities and emerging technologies. The Innovation Council of the college invited industry \u0026 academia experts to provide guidance on artificial intelligence and emerging technology skills.

The keynote speaker for the session was Associate Software Engineer \textbf{Vivek Mishra}, who delivered an in-depth presentation. {\Large \textbf{BUSINESS SECTION: TALK HELD FOR COMPUTER SCIENCE STUDENTS}}
\end{center}

\vspace{0.3cm}

\textbf{Jaipur |} A special lecture was organized by Anand International College of Engineering for Computer Science students. Industry expert and IEEE Senior Member \textbf{Vivek Mishra} was invited as the keynote speaker. The event was conducted in hybrid mode (in-person and online) organized by the college's Innovation Council.

The lecture focused on ``Bridging Industry \u0026 Academia'' and discussed contemporary challenges in Computer Science. Vivek Mishra shared detailed information about artificial intelligence, machine learning, industry trends, challenges, implementation techniques and practical applications. He explained career development opportunities, emerging technologies, agile methodologies, and modern software engineering practices.

The event saw participation from Computer Science, Information Technology, and Artificial Intelligence students. College Principal Prof. Dr. Vijay Kumar Sharma, Vice Principal Prof. Dr. Pravin Agarwal were present.
